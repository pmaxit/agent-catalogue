Chapter 4 — Delegating without soft exits

⋮⋮

The soft exit is a gift you shouldn't give

We say “let me know if you get stuck” to sound kind. The report hears: this is optional, and you are allowed to wander. Delegation fails not in the brief — it fails in the loop.

⋮⋮
▶ Research trace — 3 sources, 12m scan
[Planner] pulled: eng postmortems, delegation antipatterns

Found 2 first-hand slips: Platform team @ 11p where DRI not named → PR drifted 3 weeks; Mobile release where metric was “best effort latency” → no one blocked launch.

Takeaway: close loop needs named DRI + single metric + timestamped decision stamp. Without stamp, soft exit reopens.

⋮⋮

💡 Closed-loop delegation: One owner. One outcome metric. One timebox. Decision stamped in public — not DM.

⋮⋮

Two real slips

1. Platform migration, 11 people. “Alex and Priya pairing on cutover” — pairing without DRI means no one owns rollback call. Result: rollback hesitated 42 minutes.

2. Mobile release. Metric written as “improve launch”. No counter-metric. Team shipped improvement that regressed cold-start 18% — no one felt empowered to block.

⋮⋮
Open-loop (fails)
“Let me know if need help”
No DRI • Metric vague • Decision in DMs • Exit left open
Closed-loop (works)
Priya DRI • p95 < 800ms • Stamp by Fri 4pm
Check Wed • Public decision log • No soft exit
⋮⋮
standup_stamp.sh — decision log linebash
#!/bin/bash
# stamp decision in public
echo "$(date -Iseconds) [DECISION] DRI=priya METRIC=p95<800ms CUTOVER=2026-08-04T16:00Z" >> decisions.log
git commit -m "stamp: cutover DRI priya p95<800ms" --allow-empty
